# Image credits

The filter artwork in `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/` is based on the free
camera SVG cut files from [Plotcaster](https://www.plotcaster.com/svg/camera/).
The illustrations were recoloured, scaled, and given transparent backgrounds
for use as FilmCam filter artwork.

Plotcaster's free SVG license permits personal and commercial use and
modification, with no credit required. The original files are not redistributed
as standalone assets; these are adapted app graphics.
